# Darknet market exposure

## Description
Addresses linked to darknet markets receive payments for illegal goods and send proceeds to vendors and cash-out services. Some markets and their payment addresses have been sanctioned. Exposure is more common on Bitcoin but also appears on Ethereum through stablecoin payments.

## On-chain indicators
- Counterparty labeled as a darknet market or vendor
- Many small inbound payments followed by periodic consolidation
- Links to sanctioned services that handled market proceeds

## Example signals
- Check counterparties against sanctions and labels
- Look for many small inflows with periodic large outflows
- Check scam intel for related reports
