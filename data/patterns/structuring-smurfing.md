# Structuring and smurfing

## Description
Structuring splits a large amount into many smaller transfers to stay below reporting or review thresholds. In crypto this appears as many withdrawals of similar size, from one or more accounts, to the same destination or to a set of linked fresh addresses.

## On-chain indicators
- Repeated withdrawals just below a threshold within a short period
- Several accounts sending similar amounts to the same destination
- Destinations that consolidate the pieces soon after

## Example signals
- Check the amounts of recent inflows to the destination for clustering below round thresholds
- Count distinct senders paying the destination similar amounts
- Use account context for the account's recent withdrawals
